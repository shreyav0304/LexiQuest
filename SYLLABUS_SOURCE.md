Natural Language Processing (AI401), semester VII, teaching department AIML.
Credits: 3. Total contact hours: 40. Weekly L:T:P: 3:0:0.
CIA: 50 marks. SEE: 50 marks. Total: 100 marks. Examination: 3 hours.

Each module has 8 hours. Modules 1–4 specify RBT L1, L2, L3;
Module 5 specifies L1, L2.

The syllabus also suggests classroom activities: Text Classification Game and
Grammar Check and Correction. Its outcomes cover NLP foundations and Indian
languages, statistical language models and Paninian analysis, word-level
processing, parsing, and evaluation and ethics of IR, MT and LLMs.
